# Common US Import Tariff Reference

Quick-reference for frequently searched consumer product categories.
Use USITC HTS search for definitive rates: https://hts.usitc.gov/search

## Lookup Process

1. Identify HTS code at https://hts.usitc.gov/search?query=PRODUCT
2. Check "General" rate (column 1) — this applies to most countries
3. Check if Section 301/232 additional duties apply (China-specific mostly)
4. Germany/EU/Japan/UK/Canada/Australia = usually just the General rate

## Important Notes

- **De minimis:** Shipments valued under $800 may clear with no duty/tax (T86 entry)
- **Section 301 (China):** 25-100% additional duty on many categories — only for China-origin goods
- **Section 232 (Steel/Aluminum):** 25% — only for steel/aluminum products
- **State sales tax:** eBay/major platforms collect this regardless of origin country
- **Carrier brokerage fees:** FedEx/UPS/DHL typically charge $5-15 for customs clearance on dutiable shipments; may be waived on duty-free items
- **eBay GSP (Global Shipping Program):** Upfront import charges included — no surprises but often overcharges by 10-20%
